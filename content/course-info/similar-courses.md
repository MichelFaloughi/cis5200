## CIS 3990: Mathematics of Machine Learning

A new pilot offering at Penn covering the mathematical background CIS 5200
assumes: multivariable calculus, linear algebra, and probability and statistics.
Recommended if [Homework 0](/course-info/assessments) suggests you would benefit
from shoring up the fundamentals before or alongside this course.

## CIS 4190: Applied Machine Learning

A more applied, less theoretical introduction to ML. Heavier focus on tooling and end-to-end pipelines. Good alternative if you want practice over proofs.

## CIS 5210: Artificial Intelligence

Broader AI survey covering search, planning, logic, and probabilistic reasoning in addition to learning. Pairs well with CIS 5200 as a companion course.

## CIS 5300: Natural Language Processing

Modern NLP from word embeddings through transformer-based LLMs. Builds directly on the neural network and sequence model material in CIS 5200. Recommended follow-on.

## CIS 5450: Big Data Analytics

Data engineering and large-scale analytics. Complements CIS 5200 if you want to handle the data side of ML systems at scale.

## CIS 6200: Advanced Topics in Machine Learning

Graduate seminar going deeper into modern ML research. CIS 5200 is a recommended prerequisite.

## ESE 5450: Data Mining: Learning from Massive Datasets

ESE's counterpart focused on scalable algorithms and statistical estimation in high dimensions.
