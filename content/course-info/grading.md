## Component Breakdown

| Component | Weight | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Homeworks | 20% | HW1 through HW5; HW0 is checked for completion only |
| Oral Assessments | 25% | Two assessments, 12.5% each |
| Midterm | 25% | See the schedule for the date |
| Final | 30% | December, date TBA |

See [Assessments](/course-info/assessments) for what each component involves, and
the [schedule](/) for dates.

Note that 80% of your grade comes from the oral assessments, the midterm, and
the final, none of which allow access to language models. Keep that in mind
when deciding how much to lean on them for homework; see the
[AI policy](/course-info/policies) for details.
