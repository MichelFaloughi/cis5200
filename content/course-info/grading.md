## Component Breakdown

| Component | Weight | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Homework (HW0–HW6) | 40% | Best 6 of 7 scored |
| Midterm Exam | 25% | Wed Oct 14 |
| Final Exam | 30% | Finals Week (TBD) |
| Participation | 5% | Ed Discussion & recitation |

## Letter Grade Cutoffs

| Letter | Percentage |
|--------|------------|
| A | 93–100 |
| A− | 90–92 |
| B+ | 87–89 |
| B | 83–86 |
| B− | 80–82 |
| C+ | 77–79 |
| C | 70–76 |
| D | 60–69 |
| F | < 60 |

The course is not curved by default, but the instructor reserves the right to adjust cutoffs downward if warranted.

## Regrade Requests

Regrade requests must be submitted through Gradescope within **7 days** of the grade being released. Note that we will regrade the entire submission, so your score may go up or down.
