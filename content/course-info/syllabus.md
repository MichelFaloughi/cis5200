## Prerequisites

- Linear algebra (matrix operations, eigendecomposition)
- Multivariable calculus (gradients, partial derivatives)
- Probability & statistics (distributions, expectation, MLE)
- Programming proficiency in Python; experience with NumPy

## Topics Covered

- **Foundations:** linear algebra, probability, and optimization recap
- **Supervised learning:** linear & logistic regression, MLE, regularization
- **Margin-based methods:** SVMs and kernels
- **Tree-based methods:** decision trees, bagging, boosting
- **Learning theory:** bias–variance, PAC learning, VC dimension
- **Neural networks:** backprop, CNNs, sequence models, transformers
- **Unsupervised learning:** PCA, clustering, EM, graphical models
- **Reinforcement learning:** MDPs, policy gradients
- **Responsible ML:** fairness, interpretability, and limitations

## Recommended Reading

No single textbook is required. The following are useful references:

- *Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning* by Bishop
- *The Elements of Statistical Learning* by Hastie, Tibshirani, Friedman
- *Deep Learning* by Goodfellow, Bengio, Courville
