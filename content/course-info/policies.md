## Attendance & Participation

Lectures will not be live-streamed, but slides and notes will be posted after class. Recitations are recommended but optional. You are responsible for any material covered in class.

## Late Submissions

Each student receives a total of **5 late days** to use across all homework assignments, with a maximum of 2 late days per assignment. After late days are exhausted, late work receives a 20% penalty per day, up to 3 days late; submissions later than that receive no credit.

## Collaboration

You are encouraged to discuss problems and concepts with classmates. However, all written work and code must be your own. Do not share solutions, code, or LaTeX with other students.

## Use of AI Tools

Generative AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, GitHub Copilot, etc.) may be used to clarify concepts or debug syntax, but may **not** be used to generate solutions to homework problems. Any AI-assisted work must be disclosed in your submission.

## Academic Integrity

Violations of Penn's [Code of Academic Integrity](https://catalog.upenn.edu/pennbook/code-of-academic-integrity/) will be reported to the Office of Student Conduct. When in doubt, ask before submitting.

## Accommodations

Students requesting accommodations should contact the [Weingarten Learning Resources Center](https://www.vpul.upenn.edu/lrc/) and forward their letter to the instructor as early in the semester as possible.
