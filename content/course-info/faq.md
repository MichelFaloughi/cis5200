## Getting Started

### I am not sure I meet the prerequisites. Should I take this course?

[Homework 0](/course-info/assessments), released in the second week, exists to
answer exactly this. It is graded for completion only, so it costs you nothing
to attempt honestly. If you are comfortable with it, you will very likely do
well here. If much of it is a struggle, consider
[CIS 3990, Mathematics of Machine Learning](/course-info/similar-courses) first.

### I enrolled but I am not on Gradescope yet. Is something wrong?

No. You are added to Gradescope a few days after you enroll. If it has been
longer than that, post on Ed.

## Logistics

### Where should I ask questions?

On Ed. It is used for all course discussion, and it is the fastest way to reach
the staff. The link is in the top bar.

### Where do I find slides, notes, and recordings?

On Canvas, which holds all course materials, assignments, and grades. Lecture
recordings are also collected on Panopto; both links are in the top bar.

### Are lectures recorded?

Yes. All classes are recorded and recordings are released immediately, and you
may attend over Zoom as much as you'd like. Note that questions asked in the
Zoom chat are checked much less often than questions in the room, so Zoom is not
a great exclusive option if you like to ask questions. See
[Policies](/course-info/policies) for the full details.

### When and where are recitations?

Every week in the Berger Auditorium in Skirkanich Hall: Thursdays 2:00 to
3:00 PM, Fridays 3:30 to 4:30 PM, and Fridays 5:00 to 6:00 PM. The three
sessions cover the same material, so pick whichever fits your schedule. See the
[syllabus](/course-info/syllabus) for details.

### When are office hours?

The full weekly schedule, with locations and meeting links, is on the
[calendar](/calendar) page. You can also subscribe to it from there.

## Homework and Grading

### Where do I submit homework?

On Gradescope. The link is in the top bar.

### What tools do I need for the programming parts?

Homework programming is done in Jupyter notebooks using Python and PyTorch.
Google Colab is a fine place to run them, so you do not need a local setup.

### What is the late policy?

You have ten (10) late days for the semester, split across homeworks however you
like, no questions asked. You do not need to tell us when you are using them.
See [Policies](/course-info/policies) for what happens once they run out.

### Can I use ChatGPT on homework?

Yes, with caveats: cite it as a collaborator, say briefly how you used it, and
remember that you are responsible for what you submit. See the AI policy under
[Policies](/course-info/policies). Also keep in mind that 80% of your grade comes
from assessments where you will not have access to it.

### How do I schedule my oral assessment?

Each TA has a Calendly link for booking a slot. If none of your TA's times work,
email them and we will find another TA who can take you. See
[Assessments](/course-info/assessments).
