## Enrollment

### The course is full. Can I get off the waitlist?

Waitlist questions are handled by the CIS department, not the course staff.
Please check the department's permit request system. We cannot manually add
students.

### I am not sure I meet the prerequisites. Should I take this course?

You should be comfortable with linear algebra, multivariable calculus,
probability, and Python. HW0 is designed as a self-check: if it feels
unreasonably hard, consider taking a prerequisite course first or a more
applied alternative like CIS 4190 (see Similar Courses at Penn).
